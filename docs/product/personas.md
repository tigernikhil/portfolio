# Product Personas Documentation

## Overview
This document outlines the user personas for the Personal Technology Platform, including their needs, capabilities, and roles within the system. The personas are categorized into Visitor, Registered User, Invited User, Administrator, and Platform Owner.

## Visitor
### Persona Description
A Visitor is a user who has not registered or logged into the platform. They are interested in exploring the platform's features and services without any restrictions.

### Needs and Capabilities
- **Needs**: Access to the platform's features and services.
- **Capabilities**: Browse the platform, view public content, and access basic features without restrictions.

## Registered User
### Persona Description
A Registered User is a user who has created an account on the platform. They have access to the platform's features and services, but may have limited administrative capabilities.

### Needs and Capabilities
- **Needs**: Access to their personal content and settings, as well as the ability to interact with other users within the platform.
- **Capabilities**: Manage their account settings, view and edit their personal content, and interact with other users within the platform.

## Invited User
### Persona Description
An Invited User is a user who has been invited by a Registered User or Administrator to join the platform. They have access to the platform's features and services, but may have limited administrative capabilities.

### Needs and Capabilities
- **Needs**: Access to their personal content and settings, as well as the ability to interact with other users within the platform.
- **Capabilities**: Manage their account settings, view and edit their personal content, and interact with other users within the platform.

## Administrator
### Persona Description
An Administrator is a user who has been granted administrative privileges by the Platform Owner. They have access to all features and services of the platform, and can manage other users and their settings.

### Needs and Capabilities
- **Needs**: Full access to all features and services of the platform, including the ability to manage other users and their settings.
- **Capabilities**: Manage all users, their settings, and permissions, as well as manage the platform's settings and features.

## Platform Owner
### Persona Description
A Platform Owner is a user who has been granted the highest level of access to the platform. They have access to all features and services of the platform, and can make decisions about the platform's direction and features.

### Needs and Capabilities
- **Needs**: Full access to all features and services of the platform, including the ability to make decisions about the platform's direction and features.
- **Capabilities**: Manage all users, their settings, and permissions, as well as manage the platform's settings and features.

## Conclusion
This document provides a comprehensive overview of the user personas for the Personal Technology Platform, including their needs and capabilities. Understanding these personas is crucial for designing and implementing a production-ready platform that meets the needs of all users.
